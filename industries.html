Our Industry Expertise

We deliver specialized engineering solutions for these key sectors

Automotive & Auto Ancillaries

Design services for conventional and e-vehicles. Casting, forging, plastic, and sheet metal component design. Competency in GD&T, tolerance stacks, and electro-mechanical systems (PDU, batteries).

Agriculture & Farm Equipment

In-depth understanding of farm machinery (seeders, tillers, harvesters). Design of unmanned seeders, electric conveyors, and solar-operated threshers. Force calculations for tillage equipment.

Civil Steel Structural Design

Design, modelling, erections, and detailing experience. Compliance with AISC and other country-specific standards for civil and industrial steel structural works.

Oil & Gas

Piping and layout design, BIM, large assembly weldments, and kinematics checks for offshore equipment (cranes, drill pipes). Motor sizing and actuator selection.

Medical Equipment

User-centric design for evolving medical devices. Adherence to regulatory standards, VAVE product development for cost reduction, and support for digital health trends (AI, data analytics).

Defense

Electronic and electro-mechanical system designs for special purpose vehicles. Portable high-energy Li-ion systems, electric propulsion for land/ships, and GTV 6x6 propulsion design.

Renewable Energy

PV sizing, energy yield calcs, BESS system design for EV/Industry. Cell array C2C/C2P configs, bus bar design (IEC standards), and control logic/SCADA.
